Enforced and shown

An enforced limit refuses the request. A second project is refused with plan_limit_reached and nothing is created. A shown limit is displayed against your usage but not enforced.

Notices at 80%

Once a day, the console notifies you when a project passes 80% of a shown limit. Nothing is emailed.
